Strawberries

Strawberries have to be grown in specific climates and they have growth conditions that need to be attended!
For more information contact us on many different social media apps!
Tons of people enjoy strawberries but some do not due to allergies or just opinions!
The "seeds" on the outside are actually small fruits called achenes, while the red flesh is a, "enlarged flower receptacle". They are not true berries.
