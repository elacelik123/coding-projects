Facts
Unique Density (It Would Float): Saturn is the only planet in the solar system with a lower average density than water. It is primarily made of hydrogen and helium, and if you had a large enough ocean, Saturn would float in it. Extensive

Ring System: While other gas giants have rings, Saturn's are the most extensive and visible, stretching up to 175,000 miles away from the planet's surface. They are made of ice, rock, and dust particles.

Extremely Fast Rotation: A day on Saturn takes only 10.7 hours (the time it takes for one spin). However, it takes roughly 29.4 Earth years for Saturn to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Massive Moon Titan: Saturn has 146 confirmed moons, the most in the solar system. Its largest moon, Titan, is larger than the planet Mercury and has a thick atmosphere and lakes of methane and ethane. Hexagon Storm: At Saturn's north pole, there is a persistent, massive hexagonal-shaped, jet stream pattern that is wider than two Earths.
