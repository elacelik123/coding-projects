Drawing

Drawing is an accessible, inexpensive hobby that boosts creativity, reduces stress, and improves focus. Beginners should start with simple tools like a pencil and sketchbook, aiming for consistent, daily practice (even 10–15 minutes) rather than perfection. Focus on enjoying the process, exploring different techniques, and observing the world around you

Chess

Chess is a deeply rewarding, accessible, and lifelong hobby that enhances cognitive skills like memory, logic, and planning. As a hobby, it offers a blend of creative, social, and mental challenges, providing joy and stress relief while fostering patience and sportsmanship. It is highly engaging for all skill levels and can be played anywhere.

Gardening

Gardening is a rewarding hobby that combines physical activity with mental relaxation, offering benefits like reduced stress, improved mood, and a connection to nature. It is versatile, ranging from container gardening on balconies to large vegetable plots, and can improve home aesthetics. It also boosts physical health through, for example, light exercise and improved flexibility.
