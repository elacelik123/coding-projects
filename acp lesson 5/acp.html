What are cats??

Cats (Felis catus) are small, carnivorous mammals in the Felidae family, domesticated around 10,000 years ago from African wildcats to control rodents. Known for being agile, independent predators, they feature retractable claws, keen night vision, and flexible bodies. They are highly popular, affectionate pets that communicate through meowing, purring, and body language.

Why Cats are so different

  1. Unique Sensory and Biological Adaptations
  2. Complex, Independent Communication
  3. Instinctual Environmental Behavior
