Photography

In this hobby, you use a camera or smartphone to capture images of landscapes, people, or nature. Beyond just taking pictures, it often involves learning about lighting and composition, as well as using software to edit and improve your shots.

Chess

This is a two-player board game where the goal is to checkmate the opponent's king. It involves deep strategic thinking, memorising different openings or plays, and making split-second decisions to outmanoeuvre your opponent

Gardening

This hobby involves preparing soil, planting seeds or seedlings, and maintaining them by watering, weeding, and pruning. It is a hands-on activity where you nurture plants to grow flowers, vegetables, or herbs.
