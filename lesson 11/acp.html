Trees

  • Trees gain most of their mass from carbon dioxide in the air, not nutrients from the soil, through photosynthesis.

  • Trees communicate and share nutrients via underground fungal networks, alerting neighbors to threats like insect attacks.

  • The oldest trees, such as the Great Basin Bristlecone Pine, can live for over 5,000 years. The tallest is a 115-meter coast redwood named Hyperion.

  • A single tree can provide enough oxygen for two people annually and absorb up to 60 pounds of CO2 .
