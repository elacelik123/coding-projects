Turtles

Turtles are ancient, cold-blooded reptiles dating back over 200 million years, characterized by a protective bony shell fused to their spine and ribs

Key Facts About Turtles

Shell Anatomy: A turtle's shell is made of over 50 bones and cannot be removed, as it is part of their skeleton.
